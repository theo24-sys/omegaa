📋 How Monthly Contributions Work

  1. 1. You complete jobs and earn money through Charlady
  2. 2. At month-end, we calculate your total salary earned
  3. 3. Your 3% contribution is calculated from that total
  4. 4. You receive a reminder to pay before the 10th of next month
  5. 5. Make the payment via M-Pesa to stay active

📅 Payment Schedule

  • 📌 Month-End: Contributions calculated automatically
  • 📬 1st of Month: Payment reminder sent
  • ⏰ By 10th of Month: Payment deadline (optional but recommended)
  • ☑️ After 15th: Late payment notifications
  • ⚠️ After 30 Days: Account visibility may be reduced

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I didn't complete any jobs?

If you didn't complete any jobs in a month, no contribution is due. We only charge contributions based on jobs you've completed.

Can I receive an invoice for the payment?

Yes! Invoices are automatically emailed to you after payment. You can also download invoices from your payment history.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Missing payments may result in reduced profile visibility and job matching opportunities. We'll send reminders before any action is taken.

Is there a way to dispute a contribution amount?

If you believe your contribution was calculated incorrectly, contact support at support@charlady.co.ke with your job details.
