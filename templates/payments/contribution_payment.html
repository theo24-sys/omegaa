Frequently Asked Questions

How is the salary calculated?

Your salary is calculated based on jobs you completed in the previous month. The 3% is calculated from the total amount earned through completed jobs.

Can I skip a month?

Missing payments may result in account suspension or reduced visibility. We recommend staying current with contributions to maintain your active status.

What if I didn't earn anything?

If you didn't complete any jobs in the previous month, no contribution is due. The payment will show as waived automatically.
