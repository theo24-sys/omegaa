How to Build a Standout Profile on CHARLADY

Whether you’re a househelp aiming to land your dream gig or an employer hunting for top-tier talent, your CHARLADY profile is your golden ticket. With thousands of users connecting across Kenya, a killer profile can set you apart. Let’s walk you through pro tips to craft a profile that shines—complete with verification badges, M-Pesa payments, and more. Ready to get noticed?

1. Upload a Professional Profile Photo

First impressions matter, fam! Your profile pic is the first thing people see. Here’s how to nail it:

  • Use a high-quality, well-lit photo—think natural light or a clean setup.
  • Dress sharp (e.g., neat uniform for househelps, professional vibe for employers) and flash a friendly smile.
  • Skip group shots or busy backgrounds—keep it all about you!

Pro Tip: Employers love seeing a face they can trust, and househelps with clear pics get more hits.

2. Craft a Fire Bio

Your bio is your elevator pitch—make it short, sweet, and packed with value.

  • Househelps: Flex your skills. Example: “Certified nanny with 5+ years in childcare. Expert in cooking Kenyan dishes, cleaning, and tutoring kids. Punctual, verified, and ready to bring peace to your home!”
  • Employers: Tell househelps what you need. Example: “Seeking a live-in housekeeper in Nairobi. Must be reliable, great with laundry, and verified. Offering KSH 15,000/month + accommodation.”

Pro Tip: Mention if you’re verified (househelps) or looking for verified workers (employers)—it’s a trust booster!

3. Showcase Your Skills and Experience

Flex what you bring to the table—don’t hold back!

  • Househelps: List specifics:
    • Childcare: Nanny for newborns or teens? Say it!
    • Housekeeping: Deep cleaning, organizing, laundry—spell it out.
    • Cooking: Ugali pro? Special diets? Let ‘em know!
  • Employers: Define your gig:
    • Tasks: Cooking, cleaning, or kid-wrangling—be clear.
    • Perks: Competitive pay (e.g., KSH 200/day), weekends off, or a cozy room.

Pro Tip: Verified househelps with detailed skills get hired 2x faster on CHARLADY!

4. Get That Verification Badge

Stand out with CHARLADY’s green “Verified” badge—it’s like a VIP pass! Here’s how househelps earn it:

  • Pay the 300 KSH registration fee via M-Pesa Till Number 4567052.
  • Upload your ID, agreement form, and police clearance certificate in your profile.
  • Paste your M-Pesa confirmation code (e.g., “XXXYYYZZZ10”) when prompted.
  • Once admin verifies your payment and docs, bam—badge unlocked!

Why It Matters: Employers trust verified workers—90% of our hires go to badge holders!

5. Add Certifications and Training

Got extra cred? Show it off:

  • First Aid or CPR training? Huge win for nannies!
  • Housekeeping courses or hotel experience? Employers dig that.
  • Speak English, Swahili, or more? Flex those language skills—it’s a big plus!

Pro Tip: Certifications make you stand out. Employers love seeing trained professionals!
