CHARLADY PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: April 19, 2026

Charlady respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal data.

1 Information We Collect

Charlady may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Full name, Phone, Email
  • National ID number
  • Profile photos

Platform & Technical

  • Profiles & Job listings
  • IP address, device info
  • Usage data & activity

2 How We Use Your Information

Charlady uses personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Verify worker identity
  • Connect employers with workers
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud

3 Information Sharing

Charlady may share information with:

  • Employers reviewing worker profiles
  • Service providers assisting with verification
  • Legal authorities where required by law

Charlady does not sell personal data to third parties.

4 Data Security

Charlady implements reasonable security measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no digital platform can guarantee absolute security.

5 User Rights

Users have the right to access their personal information, request corrections, or request deletion of their account.

6 Cookies and Tracking

Charlady may use cookies and analytics tools to improve user experience, understand platform usage, and optimize performance.

7 Data Retention

Charlady retains user data only as long as necessary to operate the platform and comply with legal obligations.

8 Changes to Privacy Policy

Charlady may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated policies will be published on the platform.

9 Legal Basis for Data Processing

Charlady processes personal data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity: Data required to provide platform services
  • Legitimate Interests: Fraud prevention, security, and platform improvement
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting regulatory obligations in Kenya
  • User Consent: When explicitly requested for optional features

10 Third-Party Services & Processors

Charlady may share data with trusted service providers who assist with:

  • Payment processing (M-Pesa, payment gateways)
  • Identity verification (Didit, other verification services)
  • Cloud hosting and data storage
  • Customer support and analytics

All third-party processors are required to maintain strict data confidentiality and are prohibited from using data for their own purposes.

11 User Rights & Data Subject Rights

Users have the following rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of personal data Charlady holds about you
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of personal data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Right to Data Portability: Request data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Opt-out of marketing communications or certain processing
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how your data is used

To exercise these rights, contact support@charlady.co.ke. Requests will be processed within 30 days.

12 International Data Transfers

While Charlady operates primarily in Kenya, data may be transferred internationally for processing, storage, or service provision.

Any international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and are conducted with appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

13 Children's Privacy

Charlady does not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18 years old. If we become aware that a child's information has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

Parents or guardians who believe a child's data has been collected may contact us at privacy@charlady.co.ke.

14 Automated Decision-Making

Charlady may use automated processes to:

  • Recommend workers or jobs based on profile matching
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Flag suspicious account behavior

Users have the right to request human review of automated decisions that significantly affect them.

15 Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting personal information, Charlady will:

  • Notify affected users within 30 days
  • Provide information about the breach and steps taken
  • Recommend protective measures users should take

16 Data Protection Officer

Charlady has appointed a Data Protection Officer to oversee privacy compliance. For data protection concerns or inquiries:

Email: privacy@charlady.co.ke

Contact & Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding privacy, data use, or to exercise your data rights, contact:

General Support

support@charlady.co.ke

Privacy Officer

privacy@charlady.co.ke

Response Time: We aim to respond to all data requests within 14 business days.
