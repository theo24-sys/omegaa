CHARLADY PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

Charlady respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting personal data.

1 Information We Collect

Charlady may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Full name, Phone, Email
  • National ID number
  • Profile photos

Platform & Technical

  • Profiles & Job listings
  • IP address, device info
  • Usage data & activity

2 How We Use Your Information

Charlady uses personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Verify worker identity
  • Connect employers with workers
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud

3 Information Sharing

Charlady may share information with:

  • Employers reviewing worker profiles
  • Service providers assisting with verification
  • Legal authorities where required by law

Charlady does not sell personal data to third parties.

4 Data Security

Charlady implements reasonable security measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no digital platform can guarantee absolute security.

5 User Rights

Users have the right to access their personal information, request corrections, or request deletion of their account.

6 Cookies and Tracking

Charlady may use cookies and analytics tools to improve user experience, understand platform usage, and optimize performance.

7 Data Retention

Charlady retains user data only as long as necessary to operate the platform and comply with legal obligations.

8 Changes to Privacy Policy

Charlady may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated policies will be published on the platform.
